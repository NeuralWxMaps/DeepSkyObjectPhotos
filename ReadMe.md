This will be a collection of Deep Sky Objects that were imaged using a Seestar S50 Smart Telescope. 

The telescope has a focal length of 50 mm (about 2 inches) and a field of view around 2 degrees. The telescope collects data in 10 second increments and stacks the images automatically in the Seestar app on your phone or iPad.

Individual images can be saved and processed by third party applications, like Pixinsight of Siril, but the images in this repository have been taken taken directly from the Seestar application. 

The telescope can be operated in either Altitude/Azimuth mode or with an Equatorial Mount. Images in this repository have been collected in Alt/Az mode.
